OUR LEGACY

The Story of Courage & Sacrifice

Defence Day is observed every year on 5 September to remember the courage and sacrifices made during the 1965 war.

It is a day when the nation remembers the soldiers who stood firm to protect Pakistan and its people. Their determination became a symbol of national unity and resilience.
